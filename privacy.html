How the Pravely app handles financial data

When you use the Pravely app, we process the information you choose to enter—such as budgets, income and expense categories, debts, assets, liabilities, goals, calculator inputs, preferences, and generated reports—to save, synchronize, display, and secure your private workspace. Depending on applicable law and context, some financial information may be considered sensitive personal information.

  • Private by account. Customer financial rows are linked to the signed-in user and protected by database access rules designed to prevent one customer from accessing another customer’s information.
  • No advertising sale. We do not sell customer-entered financial data or authorize service providers to use it for their own advertising.
  • Limited operations access. Our routine support and product-health tools are designed to avoid showing budgets, balances, debts, goals, and net worth. Access may occur only when needed for security, troubleshooting you request, legal compliance, or maintaining the Service.
  • Deletion. You may permanently delete your account and customer-entered financial workspace in app Settings or contact support@pravely.com. Limited purchase, tax, fraud-prevention, security, or dispute records may be retained where reasonably necessary or legally required.
  • Payments. Stripe, Apple, or Google process payment information. Pravely receives purchase and entitlement records, but does not store full payment-card numbers.

AI-assisted financial coaching

Pravely offers an optional AI Health Coach. When you choose to use it, we send a limited summary of your financial-health calculations and the question or scenario you submit to OpenAI, our AI service provider, so it can generate educational planning guidance.

  • Data sent. The request may include your health score and summarized income, expenses, investing, debt-payment, surplus, emergency-runway, scenario, and check-in values. We do not intentionally send your name, email address, account identifier, individual debt names, transaction descriptions, receipt files, or full account records.
  • Purpose and choice. Processing is used only to answer the coaching request you initiate. AI coaching is optional; the rest of Pravely remains available if you do not use it.
  • Retention setting. Pravely requests that OpenAI not store the model response for later retrieval by setting API storage to off. Limited safety, security, and abuse-monitoring processing may still occur under the provider’s applicable terms and retention controls.
  • Human review and decisions. AI responses can be incomplete or incorrect. They are educational, are not financial, tax, legal, accounting, or investment advice, and should not be the sole basis for a significant financial decision.

10. CONTROLS FOR DO-NOT-TRACK FEATURES

 Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems and mobile applications include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature or setting you can activate to signal your privacy preference not to have data about your online browsing activities monitored and collected. At this stage, no uniform technology standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized. As such, we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals or any other mechanism that automatically communicates your choice not to be tracked online. If a standard for online tracking is adopted that we must follow in the future, we will inform you about that practice in a revised version of this Privacy Notice.

California law requires us to let you know how we respond to web browser DNT signals. Because there currently is not an industry or legal standard for recognizing or honoring DNT signals, we do not respond to them at this time.
